## Style guide

- Audience: working developers, from intermediate level. Explain *why* as well as *how*, but don't explain basics the audience already knows.
- Voice: clear, direct and friendly, in the second person ("you"). Use the active voice. No hype, and no filler like "In today's fast-paced world" or "Let's dive in".
- Paragraphs: short, 2 to 4 sentences. Use lists for steps and options.
- Use the official casing for product and library names (Angular, RxJS, Next.js, Gemini, TypeScript).
- Use American English spelling.
- Refer to the video naturally ("In the video, Ahsan shows ..."), but sparingly. The article is the main product.
